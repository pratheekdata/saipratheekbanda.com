About

I'm a Senior Analytics Engineer who has spent the last 6 years at Maersk building the data systems behind operational decisions in global shipping — from automated exception notifications that save $100K/year to KPI platforms serving 300+ colleagues across North America.

My edge is in the full stack of data work: I design the models, write the pipelines, build the automation, and care about what the metric means to the person reading it. I work primarily on Azure and Databricks, with a bias toward solutions that are clear, maintainable, and observable — built to last past the initial delivery.

Currently open to senior data engineering and analytics engineering roles, and to consulting engagements where there's a real operational problem to solve.
